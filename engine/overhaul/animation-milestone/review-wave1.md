# Wave 1 acceptance review (Fable, 2026-11-13), condensed for re-authoring

Verdict: ~280/384 distinct on paper, ~210 (55%) also drawable, reachable and with live gates.
Systemic fixes became the v2 rules (authoring-brief.md section 2a) and rep.py v2.

## Cross-cutting
- Stamped across objects (keyframe-identical): walk into it in the dark, puddle stamp, wet slip, stuck-and-POP,
  gust brace, shade squat, storm huddle, sulk, bow, present-to-nobody, argue-with-it, flamingo pose. 10-14 per object.
- Dead or always-true gates on 20 s decay fields (swing, tilt, bob, squeak, speed, echo, sway).
- Shadow wetness fields (damp/rain/water/puddle) beside the real drawn wear; every scrub ran on a clean object.
- Lines: brief samples pasted into five objects; formulas repeat ("Puddle. Mine.", "not sulking", "Nobody");
  second lines paraphrase the first; Cheese called a goose; two garbled lines.

## tire_swing (36 distinct / 25 usable)
Same act: throne_pose, hold_on_in_the_gust = sit_in_hole; sulk_at_the_post, count_swings_sitting = lean_on_post;
high_five_pass = peck_the_pendulum; sit_in_its_shade = shelter_in_the_storm; scour_hole is a third dirt clean.
Eight "in the hole" acts were drawn standing beside the tyre. twig_level touches the rope at 205 px. A puddle in a
vertical tyre's hole is impossible. 15 routines had need rows.

## toy_seesaw (28 / 24), weakest set
11 routines on the base bar, rest group = one squat under six gates. drum_the_base = jackhammer_the_base;
run_the_rain_off = heave_the_end_up; weigh_the_plank = same lift. Impossible: running through an A-frame with a
centre brace; tilting the plank by pressing the pivot or pushing a fixed leg. hush_the_wet_hinge touches the brace.
The seats are 139-211 px up: work the plank ends that come down, the base, the legs; perch on the low plank end.

## birdhouse (33 / 8): re-author from scratch
The house sits on an 87 px pole (box 93-268 px). Hatchlings reach only the pole and its foot. The `(=)` glyphs are
plates, not holes; the real opening is the sprite's own `opening` anchor. Loose slat, floor gap, right rail, spindle,
leaning/swaying house were invented. Sill-as-ground acts are impossible.

## rubber_duck (36 / 32), best of 1a
Same act: blot_the_belly_dry = dust_the_belly; two snow removals; stomp_the_ripples = seesaw drumming; four template
squats. Invented: dents, flat tail. "Patch" is the duck's wing. knocked_by_the_rock_back gated on bob before the shove.

## toy_sandbox (38 / 26), most object-specific
~12 acts need him inside the box: use perch on the sand now. Same act: tightrope_board = stud_sprint;
moat_ballet = trace_moat; drumroll_bucket = ring_bucket; redraw_blown_tracks reuses trace; squeeze_damp = squelch_stomp.
perch_shovel_grip balances on a toy handle; hold_pit_edge fires an unrelated effect; stand_shovel rights a shovel drawn upright.
Native: building in the sandbox already exists.

## dog_house (38 / 33)
Good: the CHEESE sign, bowl hockey, knock-then-answer. Same act: three sits on the sill; huddle/sulk = nap with a gate;
three leans; point_at_the_roof = shout_at_the_sign; third scrub. `scent` was a dump field. brace_in_the_gale damages the house.
Invented fly. Native: resting inside already exists.

## toy_car (35 / 30)
The gap under the car is ~44 px: no dozing or sheltering under it. ride_the_sill surfs the body edge; sit_on_the_arch
perches on a painted curve; tyre_to_tyre_hops claims tyre tops; steer_by_the_tyres holds tyres 41 columns apart.
Same act: chip_frost_off_treads = pick_tread; scrape_the_windscreen = wipe_cabin_glass; heave_against_wind_roll =
chock_the_front_tyre; wind_up_the_take_off = shove_from_behind. `lamp` a dump field; 12 tiny padding rows.
